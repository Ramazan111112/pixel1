— About the App

Professional
Pixel Art Camera

Pixera is a live retro-rendering camera that lets you adjust pixelation and color palettes in real time — while recording — with no editing required. It transforms reality into procedural pixel art, instantly.

Real-time Control

Adjust pixel density and dithering depth live, while you record.

No Post-Production

Visual transitions happen instantly, powered by the GPU — no editing needed.

Pixel Palettes

Dozens of built-in palettes — from 8-bit Nintendo to modern lo-fi aesthetics.

Export Ready

Save videos and photos directly to your camera roll in the format you need.
